### 🔬 Core Research & Engineering Domains

*   **⚡ Optical Waveguide & MicroLED Integration:** Designing custom diffraction gratings and coupling micro-displays for ultra-high brightness daylight readability.
*   **👁️ Spatial Sensor Fusion:** Developing low-latency 6-DoF tracking, eye-tracking algorithms, and gesture recognition tailored for ultra-low-power edge accelerators.
*   **🔋 Power & Thermal Management:** Architecting distributed multi-cell temple batteries and passive thermal dissipation for continuous all-day wearability.
*   **🛠️ Embedded Firmware & OS:** Building real-time operating system (RTOS) layers and spatial rendering pipelines.
